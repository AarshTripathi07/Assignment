# Research Brief: Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE)
*As of 23 September 2026 · Prepared for Super Investing Retail Community*

---

## 1. Snapshot
- **Core Business:** Indian manufacturer of power cables, wires, and conductors catering to domestic utilities, data centres, and export markets.
- **Latest Performance (Q1 FY27):** Revenue grew 18.0% YoY to ₹1,248 crore; net profit rose 5.9% YoY to ₹82 crore. EBITDA margin contracted 170 bps to 11.2% due to a 14% surge in raw copper costs.
- **Operational Health:** Order book stands at ₹3,900 crore (+23.8% YoY) with conservative net debt-to-equity of 0.35x. Last traded on the NSE at ₹1,012 post-results.

---

## 2. Bull Case (Why to be Positive)
- **Robust Order Book & Revenue Visibility:** Order book expanded to ₹3,900 crore as of June 2026 (+23.8% YoY), providing multi-quarter visibility and backing management's FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15%–17% `[Sarvottam Cables Press Release, 8 Aug 2026; IR Concall, 11 Aug 2026]`.
- **High-Margin Data Centre & Export Momentum:** Data-centre cables represent 9% of domestic revenue and are projected to double in two years; high-margin exports rose to 21% of revenue from 16% YoY `[Sarvottam Cables Press Release, 8 Aug 2026; IR Concall, 11 Aug 2026]`.
- **Near-Term Bharuch Capacity Expansion:** The ₹620 crore Bharuch plant (70% spent via internal accruals and term debt) adds ~30% capacity and is on track for Q3 FY27 commercial rollout `[Sarvottam Cables Press Release, 8 Aug 2026; IR Concall, 11 Aug 2026]`.
- **Institutional Accumulation & De-risked Pledge:** Institutional holdings rose to 26.4% (FII: 11.8%, DII: 14.6%). Promoter pledging plummeted from 35% in March 2024 to just 4.1% in June 2026, significantly reducing governance risk `[NSE Shareholding Pattern, 15 Jul 2026; Market Watch India, 18 Mar 2024]`.

---

## 3. Bear Case (Reasons for Caution)
- **Material GST Tax Demand (~56% of Quarterly Profit):** CGST Mumbai issued a demand order of ₹46.3 crore (including interest/penalty) for FY21–FY22 ITC disputes. While under appeal, this equals ~56.5% of Q1 net profit (₹82 crore), posing contingent cash-flow risk `[NSE Announcement, 2 Sep 2026]`.
- **Working Capital Stretch in Utility Receivables:** Debtor collection cycles deteriorated, with receivable days jumping from 78 to 96 days over the trailing twelve months due to delayed disbursements from two state power utilities `[IR Concall, 11 Aug 2026]`.
- **Copper Volatility & Contract Margin Lag:** A 14% spike in copper compressed EBITDA margins by 170 bps to 11.2%. Two-thirds of contracts feature price-variation clauses, but reset with a 1-to-2 quarter lag, creating interim margin exposure `[Business Daily, 9 Aug 2026; IR Concall, 11 Aug 2026]`.
- **Unregulated Tipster Hype:** Unregistered blogs and social media channels are hyping unrealistic price targets and operator claims that retail investors should disregard `[MultibaggerAlerts, 12 Aug 2026]`.

---

## 4. Open Questions (Data Conflicts & Gaps)
1. **Financial Figure Contradiction:** *Business Daily* erroneously reported Q1 revenue as ₹1,428 crore (+35%), repeated by blogs. Official exchange filings confirm revenue was actually ₹1,248 crore (+18.0%). Rely strictly on statutory filings.
2. **Contingent Liability Timeline:** What is the legal timeline for the CGST appeal against the ₹46.3 crore demand, and will the company be required to make a statutory cash pre-deposit during litigation?
3. **Discom Cash Flow Normalization:** What concrete collection milestones are in place to recover overdue receivables from the two state utilities and return debtor days back toward 78 days?
4. **Bharuch Commissioning Execution:** Can the remaining 30% of capex at Bharuch be completed without time or cost overruns before Q3 FY27 closes?
5. **Entity Disambiguation Note:** Scraped reports regarding an ₹8 lakh municipal penalty on "Sarvottam Cable Network" (Nagpur) involve an unrelated local cable TV operator owned by Suresh Patil, not NSE: SRVCABLE.
